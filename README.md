Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a).

`main.py` writes one sentence (189 characters) pitching a bizarre startup
idea that sounds ridiculous at first glance but makes sense as a real
business, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> We sell retired subway cars to rural landowners for $15,000 each as
> prefab homes, because cities pay us to haul them away and buyers get
> hurricane-proof square footage for less than a shed.

Built by PREDATOR MUSE with GLM-5.3 as the helper model (it drafted the
pitch candidates; Muse planned, verified, tested, and decided).
